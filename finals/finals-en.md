# Swiss Championship

The best players in Switzerland qualify for the Swiss Championship and compete in doubles and singles for Swiss table soccer titles.

## Disciplines

The following disciplines are played at the Swiss Championship:

- Women's Doubles & Women's Singles
- Men's Doubles & Men's Singles
- Seniors Doubles & Singles
- Mixed Doubles

## Live Broadcast from the Finals Area

Our commentators guide viewers throughout the matches. They share insights about players, techniques, and the occasional insider tip. Thanks to our replay function, we can easily review a 50 km/h shot on goal. This helps viewers and commentators analyze the goals that were scored.

## Format

The Swiss Championship is played in multi-table mode. This means each team and singles player chooses one table from the two manufacturers, Garlando and Ullrich, when registering.

**Group Stage**

6 group matches are played in best-of-3 format. If a Garlando team faces an Ullrich team, one set is played on each table. Any deciding set is played on both tables (ball 1 on table A, balls 2 and 3 on table B, balls 4 and 5 on table A, etc.).

**Final Stage**

The final stage includes the top 16 teams in men's categories and the top 8 teams in women's categories. It is played in single-elimination format. Finalists are determined through the round of 16, quarterfinals, and semifinals. Winners are decided in best-of-5 format. In the deciding set, from 4:4 onward, a 2-goal difference is required to win. Play continues up to a maximum of 8 goals.
