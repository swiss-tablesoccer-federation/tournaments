# Sports Commission

Developing Swiss table soccer together

The Sports Commission of the Swiss Tablesoccer Federation (STF) promotes the sporting development of table soccer in Switzerland. Our goal is to create fair, attractive and future-oriented conditions for national tournaments, considering the needs of players, clubs and tournament organisers. We aim to strengthen cooperation in the community, take concerns seriously and make decisions transparently and understandably.

## Our responsibilities

The Sports Commission focuses in particular on:

- **Rules and competition conditions:** Review and further development of the Individual Sport Regulations.
- **Tournament formats and game modes:** Designing fair and attractive competitions.
- **Swiss Championship:** Developing sporting conditions and qualification systems.
- **Tournament organisation:** Improving processes and cooperation with organisers.
- **Sporting development:** Considering different performance levels and development opportunities.
- **Community concerns:** Reviewing proposals and suggestions for improvement.

Our work follows applicable regulations, sporting needs and the organisation's implementation possibilities.

## Proposals and improvement suggestions

Do you have an idea or wish to propose a change to the existing rules?

We welcome constructive proposals from the community.

**Community proposals may only be submitted through an STF member club.**

Please send your proposal to the responsible person in your club. The club reviews the proposal and submits it to the Sports Commission.

1. Which topic or existing rule does the proposal concern?
2. What specific change is proposed?
3. Why is the change sensible or necessary?
4. What effects could the change have?

## Where should proposals be sent?

Clubs may submit proposals to:

[sportkommission@swisstablesoccer.ch](mailto:sportkommission@swisstablesoccer.ch)

The Sports Commission reviews proposals against the regulations, sporting impact and organisational feasibility.

**Submission does not automatically mean that a proposed change will be implemented.**
