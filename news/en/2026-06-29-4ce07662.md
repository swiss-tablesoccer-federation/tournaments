# Swiss Tablesoccer League 2026

*29 Jun. | Written by Lukas Baertsch*

The [Swiss Tablesoccer League (STL) 2026](https://www.swisstablesoccer.ch/swiss-tablesoccer-league) is over. Last weekend, around 250 players gathered in Wohlen near Bern to compete for the coveted titles across several divisions. TFC Luzern secured the title of Swiss champion in the Super League, while TFC Bern Women 2 prevailed in the Women's League. Below is a short summary of the results. Detailed rankings can be found [here](https://www.swisstablesoccer.ch/s/Schlussrangliste_STL_2026.pdf).

#### Super League

- TFC Luzern 1 (Champions League)

- TFC Zurich 1 (Champions League)

- TFC Laupen 1

#### Challenge League

- Bern Ballers (promoted to the Super League)

- Selezione Ticino 1 (promoted to the Super League)

- CFT Jura Seeland

#### Promotion League

- TFC Seetal 1 (promoted to the Challenge League)

- TFC Zurich 2 (promoted to the Challenge League)

- Selezione Ticino 2

#### Tablesoccer League (4th division)

- TGB 4 (promoted to the Promotion League)

- TFC Seetal 2 (promoted to the Promotion League)

- TFC Zurich "Training Mit"

#### Women's League

- TFC Bern Women 2 (Champions League)

- TFC Zurich Women

- TFC Seetal Women

Our sincere thanks go to TFC Bern for the great organization and execution of the tournament. We are already looking forward to Swiss Tablesoccer League 2027.
