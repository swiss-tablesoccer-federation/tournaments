# Registrations STL 2026 / Cup Final

*4 Jun. | Written by Lukas Baertsch*

We are pleased to announce that 34 teams have registered in the Open [Swiss Tablesoccer League (STL)](https://www.swisstablesoccer.ch/swiss-tablesoccer-league). As a result, there will be four divisions ([Super League](https://app.tablesoccer.org/p/6RVO9Z/c/4596), [Challenge League](https://app.tablesoccer.org/p/6RVO9Z/c/4597), [Promotion League](https://app.tablesoccer.org/p/6RVO9Z/c/7530), and [1st Tablesoccer League](https://app.tablesoccer.org/p/6RVO9Z/c/4598)). The field is completed by four registrations for the [Women's League](https://app.tablesoccer.org/p/6RVO9Z/c/4599). We are now looking forward to the event on 27 and 28 June in Wohlen near Bern and already warmly thank TFC Bern.

In addition, the semi-finals of the Swiss Tablesoccer Cup have been played. In the [Cup final](https://app.tablesoccer.org/p/ZMGOLZ/c/4278), TFC Luzern and Foosballeur now face each other, with Foosballeur having home advantage. The cup final must be played by 31 October.

We receive three starting spots for the Men's World Champions League (WCL). The first- and second-placed teams in STL will qualify for WCL. The additional spot goes to the cup winner (any replacements come via STL). The winning team of the Women's League also qualifies for WCL.
