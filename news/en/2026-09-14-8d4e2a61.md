# Swiss Tablesoccer League 2027

*14 Sept. | Written by Lukas Baertsch*

We are pleased to announce that the next __Swiss Tablesoccer League in 2027__ will also take place in Wohlen near Bern. The planned dates are __5 and 6 June__ – many thanks already to TFC Bern.

We would also like to point out that the first Cup Final will take place on 26 September 2026. In Martigny, Foosballeur will face TFC Luzern.

The organizing committee of the Swiss Tablesoccer League
