# The first Swiss Tablesoccer Cup is history

*12 Oct. | Written by Hannes Wallimann*

Congratulations to TFC Luzern on winning the Swiss Tablesoccer Cup. They beat Foosballeur in a hard-fought final.

The final place in the World Champions League has now also been decided. TFC Laupen, third in the Swiss Tablesoccer League, takes the spot.

We are pleased that the clubs organized the event so successfully. Thanks also to SwissBioPool for sponsoring the trophy. We will decide in the coming weeks whether the competition will continue next season and communicate our decision accordingly.

For the Swiss Tablesoccer League organizing committee,  
Hannes and Esther
