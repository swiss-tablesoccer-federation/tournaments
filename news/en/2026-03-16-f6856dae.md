# The Quarter-Finals in the Swiss Tablesoccer Cup Are Over

*16 Mar. | Written by Florian Wahl*

The quarter-finals in the Swiss Tablesoccer Cup are over.

The semi-finals are now next. In the first semi-final, TFC Luzern and TFC Laupen will face each other, with TFC Laupen having home advantage.

In the second semi-final, Foosballeur will play TFC Zurich, with TFC Zurich having home advantage.

The semi-finals must be played by 30 July.
