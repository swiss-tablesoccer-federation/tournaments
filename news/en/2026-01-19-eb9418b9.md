# Draw for the Swiss Tablesoccer Cup 2026

*19 Jan. | Written by Florian Wahl*

The first round of the Swiss Tablesoccer Cup was drawn at the STS tournament in Lucerne. In this inaugural season, six teams are taking part. The quarter-finals must be played by 30 April.

In the first quarter-final, TFC Luzern and TFC Seetal will face each other, with TFC Seetal having home advantage. The winner will play TFC Laupen (bye) in the semi-final.

In the second quarter-final, TFC Zurich will face TFC Freiburg Sense, with TFC Freiburg Sense also having home advantage. The winner will play Foosballeur in the semi-final.

Whether the winner of the Swiss Tablesoccer Cup qualifies for the World Champions League is currently being clarified with the ITSF.
