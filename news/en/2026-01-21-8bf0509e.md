# Swiss Tablesoccer League 2026

*21 Jan. | Written by Florian Wahl*

We are pleased to invite the club presidents to this year's Swiss Tablesoccer League (STL), taking place on 27 and 28 June in Wohlen near Bern.

The invitation and the updated regulations are published [here](https://www.swisstablesoccer.ch/swiss-tablesoccer-league).

We also look forward to applications from interested clubs for STL 2027. Interest can be communicated by club presidents to the STL officials.

We look forward to many registrations for STL 2026.
