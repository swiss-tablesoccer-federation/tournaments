# Adjustment to Prize Money Distribution

*16 Feb. | Written by IT Swisstablesoccer*

Following the latest changes to the ITSF tournament guidelines dated 4 January 2026, the ITSF requires us to adjust prize money distribution at tournaments with ITSF status (250, 500, 750, 1000). The STF therefore guarantees prize money of CHF 2,900 for all STS tournaments in the 2026 season with immediate effect. Distribution will be adjusted accordingly as shown.

Prize money distribution at STS tournaments

The ITSF tournament guidelines no longer allow the distribution model previously used by STF. STF has therefore decided to switch to a model with guaranteed prize money for the following reasons.

- Organizing clubs receive a guaranteed share of CHF 1,100. This provides planning security.

- Other models would have required introducing a tournament fee. This is not compatible with STF's licensing model, as the annual STF license is deliberately an all-inclusive package.

- Uniform prize money promotes equal treatment of STS tournaments.

- Over the season, the changes result in only minor differences compared with the current model (60-20-20). Any surplus goes into the Swiss Championship pot.

Note: As of today, these changes have not yet been incorporated into the financial regulations. This will be aligned shortly.
