# Developing Swiss table soccer together

*22 Oct. | Written by Beatrice Berner*

What is going well in Swiss table soccer? Where is greater clarity needed? And which ideas are waiting to be heard? As the new Sports Commission of the Swiss Tablesoccer Federation, we would like to start a conversation with you about these questions.

We took office on 10 September 2026. The Sports Commission brings together six people who share your passion for table soccer: Beatrice Berner, Luca Freiburghaus, Stefan Knutti, Michael Köhler, Pascal Salzgeber and Mike Schrepfer. We want to give something back to the community and develop our sport together with you.

**Listening comes first**

Anyone who plays in tournaments or is involved in a club sees first-hand what works and where everyday challenges arise. We want to include these experiences in our work. That is why we have prepared a short survey: What is on your mind? What would you like to see? And which topics do you think we should address?

Please take a few minutes to share your thoughts. Your feedback will help us better understand the community's concerns and set priorities for our work.

**An idea can become a formal proposal**

Perhaps you already have a suggestion you would like to put forward. **Proposals to the Sports Commission can only be submitted through a club.** Please therefore contact your club with your concern so that it can submit the proposal on your behalf.

Clubs can send new proposals, as well as previously submitted proposals that have not yet received a response, to [sportkommission@swisstablesoccer.ch](mailto:sportkommission@swisstablesoccer.ch).

To make submissions easier, we have prepared a proposal template in German, French and Italian and sent it to club presidents by email. Clubs can also request it from us directly if needed.

**Let's talk in person**

In addition to written feedback, direct exchange is important to us. Please save **22 November 2026**: on that day, we invite you to open discussions with the Sports Commission. There will be room for your questions, ideas and concerns. Further information about the time and place will be sent to clubs later.

We look forward to hearing your perspectives and shaping the next steps for Swiss table soccer together.

Your Sports Commission
