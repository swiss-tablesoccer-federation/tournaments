### What is the Swiss Tablesoccer Cup?

In the Swiss Tablesoccer Cup, teams compete for their club and fight for the cup title. Every STF member club can register a team with at least 6 players.

Unlike the Swiss Tablesoccer League, the Cup is played in a *knockout format* from the start. The matchups are drawn at random. The winner advances to the next round; the loser is eliminated.

Teams organize their matches independently, with home advantage going to the lower-ranked club.

The team that wins every knockout round wins the *Swiss Tablesoccer Cup* and can call itself Swiss Cup Champion. With a bit of luck, qualification for the ITSF European Champions League may even be possible.
