### What is the Swiss Tablesoccer League?

Who will become STL club **Swiss Champion**?

In the Swiss Tablesoccer League, **teams** compete for their club. Each team has 6–9 players (open league) or 3–6 (women's league).  
Clubs can enter multiple teams.

Matches are played in **multi-table format**. If two teams choose different home tables, play takes place on both tables and sides are switched after each set. The **group stage** is used to create the ranking. The best teams play for the title or promotion, the last ones against relegation. The **final stage** is played in single-elimination format.

The **Swiss Champion** (and further teams depending on the number of teams) qualifies for the [**ITSF European Champions League**](https://www.tablesoccer.org/page/itsf-european-champions-league).
