# Anti-Doping

## Together for clean sport

Swiss Tablesoccer Federation is fully committed to doping-free sport, supports anti-doping prevention, and maintains active cooperation with the Swiss Sport Integrity foundation. Consistent compliance with anti-doping rules protects athletes, the federation, and table soccer. The leadership of Swiss Tablesoccer Federation places strong emphasis on athletes, coaches, team leaders, and medical support staff knowing the rules and standing up for clean table soccer.

## What is considered doping?

Not only prohibited substances in the body are banned, but also, for example, possession of such substances, refusal of a control, or whereabouts failures, according to the Swiss Olympic Doping Statute.

Learn more about the [eleven anti-doping rule violations](https://www.sportintegrity.ch/statut).

![What is considered doping?](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/6797790a8025010384cf53f2/868ff0d8-56be-4d5b-8879-62b248822b06/02_was-versteht-man-unter-doping.jpg?format=300w)

## Athletes are responsible

The principle of strict liability is the foundation of anti-doping prevention. It states that athletes are solely responsible for ensuring that no prohibited substances enter their bodies and that no prohibited methods are used.

Learn more about [rights and responsibilities](https://www.sportintegrity.ch/rechteundpflichten).

![Responsibility in sport](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/6797790a8025010384cf53f2/e6719429-b9f5-4a99-aa1c-c6f6394abef7/03_athleten-verantwortung.jpg?format=300w)

## Always check medication

Before taking any medication, athletes must always check whether it contains prohibited substances. The Global DRO medication check provides this information quickly and easily via [www.sportintegrity.ch](https://www.sportintegrity.ch/) or through the mobile app.

[Download now](https://www.sportintegrity.ch/medikamente)!

## Supplement risk

Supplements and nutritional products may be contaminated with prohibited substances. They therefore carry the risk of an unintentional anti-doping violation. Great care is needed when selecting products to reduce this risk.

[More information](https://www.sportintegrity.ch/supplemente) about supplement-related doping risks.

![Supplement risk](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/6797790a8025010384cf53f2/363a48e5-9ead-440c-9651-92586f0713d3/05_dopingrisiko-supplemente.jpg?format=300w)

## Doping controls - how they work

Doping controls can take place anytime and anywhere. Athletes are required to comply with test notifications and instructions from doping control personnel.

Details about the [doping control process](https://www.sportintegrity.ch/kontrollablauf).

## Report suspicions

Potential signs of doping violations may seem small, but they can be highly important for investigations. Your report can make a decisive contribution to protecting clean sport. Anonymous or not, all information is handled confidentially.

[Report suspicions](https://www.sportintegrity.ch/meldung) to help protect clean sport.
